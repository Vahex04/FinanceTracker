FinanceTracker Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

FinanceTracker is a personal budgeting app for iPhone (bundle ID com.vaheovsepian.FinanceTracker). It reads your financial data only to show you where your money goes. It works with a FinanceTracker server that you run yourself, either on your own computer or on a cloud virtual machine that you rent and control, so your financial data is stored on systems you control, not on servers run by the developer. FinanceTracker has no sign-up, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs, and it never sells, rents, or shares your data for advertising. Access is read-only: FinanceTracker cannot move money, make payments, or change anything at your bank.

Information the app accesses

FinanceTracker accesses only the financial information you choose to connect or import, plus what you type into the app.

  • Banks and credit cards, through Plaid: account names and types, the last digits of account numbers, balances, and transactions (date, amount, merchant or description, the bank's category, and pending status). You sign in on your bank's own page inside Plaid Link. FinanceTracker never sees your bank username or password.
  • Apple Card, Apple Cash, and Apple Savings, through Apple FinanceKit: account names and types, balances, and transactions (date, amount, description, merchant name, merchant category code, and status). You grant access in the iOS permission prompt.
  • Statement files you import (CSV): the dates, descriptions, amounts, and categories in the file you pick.
  • What you enter: categories you assign, notes, category rules, and monthly budget targets.

FinanceTracker does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or device identifiers. The Face ID lock is handled by iOS; the app receives only whether authentication succeeded, never your face data.

How information is used

Your information is used only to provide FinanceTracker's budgeting features to you: showing balances and transactions across your accounts, sorting transactions into spending categories and remembering the categories you choose, recognizing transfers between your own accounts so they aren't counted as spending, proposing and tracking monthly budget targets, and exporting your own data when you ask.

It is never used for advertising, marketing, credit decisions, lending, profiling, or training machine-learning models, and it is never sold, rented, or traded.

Where information is stored and how it is protected

Your financial data is stored in places you control: your FinanceTracker server (your computer or your own cloud virtual machine), its backups, and the app on your iPhone.

  • Your server: transactions, accounts, categories, and budgets are kept in a database in a folder only the server's own account can open. The bank access tokens Plaid issues are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are saved.
  • Backups: if you turn on backups, the server keeps a nightly copy of its database (the last 14) readable only by its administrator, and your Mac can keep a copy of the newest ones (the last 30) in a folder only your user account can open.
  • Your iPhone: the app keeps a copy for offline viewing. It is protected by iOS data protection and, when you turn it on, a Face ID or passcode lock that also hides balances in the app switcher.
  • In transit: the app talks only to your own server, over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Plaid's and Apple's connections to your banks are encrypted by those services.
  • Access control: every request to your server must carry a long random access token that only your app holds.

Who information is shared with

FinanceTracker shares information only with the two services below, and only to provide its features.

  • Plaid connects to the banks you choose and returns your account and transaction data. What you give Plaid when you link a bank is governed by Plaid's End User Privacy Policy. FinanceTracker sends Plaid only the tokens needed to fetch your data.
  • Anthropic (optional, off unless an API key is set) suggests a category for a merchant FinanceTracker hasn't seen before. It receives only the merchant name, amount, and date of that transaction, never account names or numbers, balances, notes, or your identity.

Apple FinanceKit data is never sent to Plaid or Anthropic. FinanceTracker does not share information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Apple Wallet data (FinanceKit)

Data from Apple Card, Apple Cash, and Apple Savings is read only with your permission, used only for your budget, and never leaves your own devices.

  • Read-only: FinanceTracker only reads accounts, balances, and transactions. It does not create, change, or delete anything in Wallet.
  • Your devices only: FinanceKit data goes from your iPhone to your own FinanceTracker server and nowhere else. It is not sent to Plaid, Anthropic, or the developer.
  • Only for your budget: it is used only for the features described above, never for advertising, marketing, credit or lending decisions, or data brokering.
  • You stay in control: you can withdraw access at any time in the iPhone Settings app, under the app's privacy permissions. Removing the Apple connection in FinanceTracker deletes its transactions from your server.

Export, retention, and deletion

Your information is kept until you delete it, and you can delete any of it at any time.

  • Remove one account: swipe the connection in Accounts and tap Remove. FinanceTracker asks Plaid to revoke its access, then deletes that connection's transactions from your server and phone. If Plaid can't be reached, you're told, and FinanceTracker keeps only the encrypted access token, retrying until Plaid confirms.
  • Delete everything: use the server's delete-all-data function (it revokes every bank connection and pauses Apple Wallet syncing until you turn it on again), or delete the FinanceTracker data folder and remove the app from your iPhone.
  • Backups: deleted data can remain in existing backups until they are replaced (about 14 days on the server and 30 days on your Mac), or you can delete the backup files yourself.
  • Export: you can export all your transactions as a CSV file at any time.
  • Plaid: to see or manage what Plaid holds, use Plaid Portal at my.plaid.com.

Children

FinanceTracker is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds data collection, analytics, advertising, or a third-party service, this policy will be updated with a new effective date before that version is released. A change will never allow your financial data to be sold or used for advertising.
